tia-fpga rev B - fabrication notes
==================================

FPGA replacement for the Atari 2600 TIA (C010444).
https://github.com/lrrosa/tia-fpga
Copyright 2026 Leonardo Roman da Rosa - CERN-OHL-S-2.0

BOARD
  Size              61.6 x 34.54 mm (rectangular, no cutouts, no mounting holes)
  Layers            2 (F.Cu / B.Cu)
  Thickness         1.6 mm FR4 (standard)
  Copper            1 oz / 35 um
  Surface finish    HASL or ENIG, either is fine
  Solder mask       both sides, any colour
  Silkscreen        both sides, white

CAPABILITY REQUIRED
  Min track width   0.18 mm  (7 mil)
  Min clearance     0.13 mm  (5.1 mil)
  Min drill         0.30 mm  (vias)
  Largest drill     1.00 mm  (through-hole connector pads)
  All within standard low-cost capability. No controlled impedance, no blind or
  buried vias, no castellations.

ASSEMBLY NOTE (not the fab's problem, but it matters)
  J2 and J3 mount on the BACK with their pins facing DOWN - they plug into the
  TIA's DIP-40 socket. J1 and J4 are sockets on the FRONT, facing UP. Use ROUND
  MACHINED pins for J2/J3: square header pins damage a DIP socket.

  The Tang Nano 9K goes in component side UP with its USB-C end on the left,
  pin 1 of both its rows at that end. J1 takes the module's J6 row (the one
  with 5V, GND and 3V3) and J4 takes J5. The rows are 22.86 mm apart, from
  Sipeed's own dimension drawing.

  U5, U6-U9 (SOT-23-6), C7-C10 and D1 sit in the top margin of the front. Their
  designators are on the BACK silkscreen, right behind each part: there is no
  room for them beside the parts.

  The back silkscreen also carries the licence (CERN-OHL-S v2), the author and
  the project's address, between the two rows of TIA pins. CERN-OHL-S v2 asks
  for that notice to travel with the board, so please keep it.

  ORIENTATION: the back carries a half moon at the end where the TIA's pin 1
  and pin 40 sit, the way the chip's own package is keyed, plus the pin numbers
  1, 20, 21 and 40 beside the corner pads. The front repeats 1 and 40.
